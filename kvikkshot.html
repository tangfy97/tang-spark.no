Kvikkshot

Screenshots from video, in a blink.

Drop in a video and the screenshots are there before you can look away. Keep the ones you want, export them, or turn them into one image to share. Kvikk is Norwegian for quick.

PlatformmacOS 14, Apple silicon & Intel
PriceFree
NetworkNone at all
LanguagesEN, 简体, 繁體

What it does

  • By interval or countOne shot every few seconds, or N shots spread across the video.
  • Fast or preciseKeyframe mode skims an hour of 4K in seconds; precise mode is exact to the frame.
  • Many videosDrop a batch; each gets its own folder on export.
  • Share imageOne sheet with columns, light or dark, timestamps and video details.
  • Leaves nothing behindCached frames are deleted on Back, on quit and at next launch.

Get Kvikkshot

Signed and notarized by Apple. Open the disk image and drag Kvikkshot to Applications. Also coming to the Mac App Store.
