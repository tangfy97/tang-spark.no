Spolo

Photos and videos, one video.

Drop in clips from your phone, camera or screen, in any mix of shapes and formats. Put them in order, trim the ends and export one MP4 or MOV, right on your Mac. Spole is Norwegian for a reel of film.

PlatformmacOS 14 or later
PriceFree, Apache 2.0
NetworkNone at all
LanguagesEN, 简体, 繁體

What it does

  • Any mixPhotos and videos in any shape and format. Upright phone videos, HDR and clips without sound all come out right.
  • In orderDrag to reorder, or sort by date taken, file name or the order you added them.
  • Each clipTrim, change the speed with the pitch kept, mute and rotate. Set how long each photo stays.
  • One shapeAuto, 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or 4:5, with black bars or filled and cropped.
  • Fast and privateNo upload, no watermark, no limits. Matching clips are joined without re-encoding.
