Mosaikk

Photo collages, shaped by hand.

Pick one of over 200 layouts, drop in your photos and drag the borders between them until it looks right. Add words, stickers and a background, and export a picture at any size, right on your Mac. Mosaikk is Norwegian for mosaic.

PlatformmacOS 14 or later
PriceFree, Apache 2.0
NetworkNone at all
LanguagesEN, 简体, 繁體

What it does

  • LayoutsGrids, feature layouts and mosaics for 1 to 25 photos, shaped frames, and freeform collages where photos lie loose like prints.
  • Drag the bordersResize cells by dragging the lines between them. Split, delete or add cells, or make them all even again.
  • Each photoDrag to choose the crop, zoom, turn and flip; drag onto another cell to swap. 16 filters and 12 adjustments.
  • Words and stickersAny font on your Mac with outline, shadow, glow and highlight, typed on the collage. Shapes, badges and emoji.
  • Any sizeColours, gradients, patterns or a blurred photo behind. Social, print and custom sizes; PNG, JPEG or HEIC.

Get Mosaikk

Free. Signed and notarized downloads are on GitHub; open the disk image and drag Mosaikk to Applications. Coming soon to the Mac App Store.
