MaskCopy

Share your ID, not all of it.

Landlords, employers, hotels and agencies often ask for a copy of your ID. Most of them don't need your personal number, document number, photo or the machine-readable zone. MaskCopy makes a copy with those parts covered, marked with who it's for, and never sends your document anywhere.

PlatformiPhone, iOS 18 or later
PriceFree, Apache 2.0
NetworkNo network code
Documents27 countries

What it does

  • Scan or importUse the camera, or pick a photo or a PDF. MaskCopy straightens the document for you.
  • Finds what to hidePersonal and document numbers, the machine-readable zone, the card access number, addresses, barcodes and the photo. Personal numbers are checked by their check digits.
  • You decideEvery suggestion is a box you can see. Turn a kind off, move a box, adjust its corners or add your own.
  • Says who it's forThe recipient, the purpose and the date are written across every page.
  • Painted, not blurredCovered parts are painted over. JPG and PDF carry no location or camera details.
  • Stays on your iPhoneApple's on-device recognition. No account, no analytics, no uploads, no history.

Documents

Passports, national ID cards, residence permits and driving licences from 27 countries are recognised field by field: the Netherlands, Germany, France, the United Kingdom, Italy, Spain, Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Belgium, Poland, Ireland, Switzerland, Austria, Portugal, the United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Japan, South Korea, Singapore and Malaysia. Passports from other countries still have their machine-readable zone found.

Recognition varies by document version and photo, so check every box before you share.
