What we optimize for.

We prefer practical interfaces over loud presentation, strong defaults over complex setup, and transparent business models over hidden tradeoffs.

Focused scope

Products should solve a real repeated problem, then stay disciplined as they grow.

Respectful data use

Local-first storage is preferred where it fits. Cloud and accounts should be optional unless clearly necessary.

Launch-quality craft

Documentation, support pages, subscriptions, and product copy should match the actual app behavior.
