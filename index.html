First product, launch ready.

Echoknot is the current TangSpark focus: a native iOS language-memory app with a free local baseline and a production subscription path.

Echoknot for iOS

Capture words from real life, enrich them with user-triggered AI, and review them through short Echo stories. The app stays local by default; Premium adds higher AI quota and optional cloud backup.

Open product page Support, privacy, terms

Production architecture

StoreKit subscription, Firebase only for opted-in Premium sync, and a Netlify Qwen proxy so AI provider keys never ship in the app bundle.

Design principles that survive scale.

Our apps should stay readable when data grows, explain paid features plainly, and avoid forcing accounts where local storage is enough.

Dense, not heavy

Interfaces are built for repeated use with compact controls, stable spacing, and fast scanning.

Privacy by default

Local-first storage remains the baseline. Cloud features are opt-in and tied to clear user value.

Transparent AI limits

Free usage is generous enough to learn; Premium quota supports heavier daily workflows.
