Rydd

Every file, back where it belongs.

Rydd lives in the menu bar and keeps the folders you choose tidy. It learns from the folders you already have, suggests where each new file goes, and moves only what you confirm. Rydd is Norwegian for tidy up.

PlatformmacOS 26 or later
PriceFree, Apache 2.0
NetworkNone at all
LanguagesEN, 简体, 繁體

What it does

  • Your foldersPick what to tidy, such as Downloads and Desktop. Nothing is read before you do.
  • Learns your systemSuggestions follow the folders you already have, not a fixed scheme.
  • Review firstEvery move is listed. Tick, tidy, and undo if you change your mind.
  • Never loses a fileNothing is overwritten, and deleting always means the Trash.
  • On-device AIOptional sorting with Apple Intelligence runs offline and only sees file names.
