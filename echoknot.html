Built for thousands of knots.

The latest Wordbook design avoids endless scrolling by separating browsing, lookup, and full-library management.

Explore, Search, All

Three modes let users browse recent learning, jump directly to a word, or manage the full collection.

Better filters

Date filters include Today, Recent 3 days, Recent week, and Recent month, plus part-of-speech and first-letter filters.

Floating navigation

The iOS app uses a Liquid Glass-style bottom island with content padding so lower controls stay reachable.

Most features stay free.

Premium is designed for heavier learners who want more AI usage and cloud backup. It is not required for normal local learning.

Free: 30AI auto-fill/detail refresh credits per day.

Purchase

Premium uses App Store auto-renewable subscription product com.tangspark.echoknot.premium.monthly.

Voucher

Free Premium trials should use App Store subscription Offer Codes for the same product.

Sync

Only Premium users see Sign in with Apple for optional Firebase backup/sync. Free users remain local-only by default.

Production-safe service boundaries.

The app does not ship provider secrets. Production AI and cloud sync are isolated behind platform-native controls.

AI proxy

iOS posts to https://tang-spark.no/echoknot/api/ai/chat. The Netlify function calls Qwen's compatible chat-completions endpoint.

Firebase only when needed

Firestore is used only after Premium entitlement, user choice, and Sign in with Apple.

Local export

Users can export JSON/PDF from Settings and Dashboard, even without Premium.
