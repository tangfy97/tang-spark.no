Getting started

  1. Open Mosaikk: a new collage with three empty cells is ready. Pick another layout on the left if you like; the list shows layouts for as many photos as you have added.
  2. Click a + in a cell, choose File › Add Photos…, or drag pictures from the Finder or Photos onto the window. They fill the empty cells in order.
  3. Drag the lines between photos to resize them, and drag inside a photo to choose what shows. Add text, stickers and a background from the sidebar, then choose File › Export….

Questions

How do I swap two photos?

Drag a photo out of its cell and drop it on another. Dragging inside the cell moves the photo within it instead.

Why do several borders move together?

Borders that line up across the whole grid, such as a column line, move together so it stays straight. Hold ⌥ (Option) while dragging to move just one.

Which picture formats can I add?

Anything macOS can open: JPEG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF, WebP, AVIF, GIF, BMP and RAW photos.

Where are my collages kept?

A collage is a document: choose File › Save to keep it as a .mosaikk file wherever you like. The file holds copies of your photos, so it still opens if the originals move, and on another Mac. Until you save, Mosaikk keeps untitled collages for you, and they come back after you quit.

Does Mosaikk take up space over time?

No. Previews are kept in memory only. Temporary files are removed after use, and copies of untitled collages that no window will open again are cleaned up when Mosaikk starts.

How large can I export?

Up to 12,000 pixels on the longer side, as PNG, JPEG or HEIC. PNG and HEIC keep a transparent background.

Is there a watermark or a limit?

No watermark. A collage can hold up to 500 pictures.

How do I update?

The Mac App Store version updates through the App Store. In the downloaded version, choose Help › Check for a New Version…; it opens this website in your browser, because Mosaikk itself never goes online.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit Mosaikk and drag it from Applications to the Bin. Its settings and any untitled collages are in ~/Library/Containers/com.feiyangtang.Mosaikk, which you can delete too. Collages you saved stay where you saved them.

Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026. This policy covers the Mac App Store version and the version downloaded from GitHub.

Mosaikk collects no data. It has no network access: the App Sandbox does not let it connect to the internet. There are no accounts, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or update checks, and no third-party code.

Mosaikk reads the pictures you add, to show them and put them in your collage. A saved collage is a .mosaikk file in the place you choose, holding unchanged copies of the pictures you added so it can be edited later. Their metadata, such as the date or place a photo was taken, stays in those copies; exported images don't include it.

Photos you add from your Photos library are chosen in the system's photo picker, so Mosaikk never has access to the library itself.

Mosaikk writes files only where you ask, and keeps untitled collages in its own folder until you save or delete them. Links in the Help menu open in your browser, outside Mosaikk. Check for a New Version tells this website which version you have, so it can say whether a newer one exists.

Mosaikk is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children. Questions about privacy: support@tang-spark.no.
